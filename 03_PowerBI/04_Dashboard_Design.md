\# Dashboard Design



\## Design Objective



The dashboard was designed to provide a clean, interactive, and business-focused reporting experience. The layout emphasizes readability, consistency, and easy navigation while allowing users to explore sales performance across multiple business dimensions.



\---



\# Design Principles



The dashboard was designed using the following principles:



\- Maintain a consistent layout across all report pages.

\- Use a limited color palette to improve readability.

\- Place KPIs at the top for immediate visibility.

\- Use interactive visuals instead of static reports.

\- Maintain consistent fonts, spacing, and alignment throughout the dashboard.

\- Minimize visual clutter while maximizing business insights.



\---



\# Theme



\## Primary Colors



\- Dark Blue – KPI Titles

\- Light Blue – Revenue Trends

\- Orange – Customer Highlights

\- Green – Positive Growth

\- Red – Negative Growth

\- White Background



These colors were selected to improve readability while maintaining a professional business appearance.



\---



\# Typography



Font Family



\- Segoe UI



Font Sizes



\- Page Title : 22–24 pt

\- KPI Title : 11–12 pt

\- KPI Value : 22–28 pt

\- Visual Title : 14–16 pt

\- Table Text : 10–11 pt



\---



\# Navigation



The dashboard is organized into six report pages:



1\. Executive Overview

2\. Customer Intelligence

3\. Customer Segmentation

4\. Product Intelligence

5\. Time Intelligence

6\. Business Insights \& Recommendations



Each page focuses on one business area while maintaining a consistent design.



\---



\# Filters



The report uses slicers to allow users to interactively filter the data.



Available filters include:



\- Country

\- Year



These filters automatically update all visuals on the current report page.



\---



\# KPI Cards



All KPI cards follow the same structure:



\- KPI Title

\- KPI Value

\- Consistent formatting

\- Minimal background styling



This consistency helps users compare metrics quickly.



\---



\# Charts



Different chart types were selected based on the analytical requirement.



| Chart Type | Purpose |

|------------|---------|

| Line Chart | Revenue trends over time |

| Column Chart | Product and country comparisons |

| Bar Chart | Customer and product rankings |

| Donut Chart | Customer segment distribution |

| Matrix/Table | Detailed product and customer analysis |



\---



\# Tables



Tables were used only where detailed records were required.



Formatting includes:



\- Alternate row shading

\- Conditional formatting

\- Proper alignment

\- Top 20 filtering where applicable



\---



\# User Experience



The dashboard was designed to allow users to:



\- Understand business performance quickly.

\- Navigate between report pages easily.

\- Filter information dynamically.

\- Identify important trends without excessive scrolling.

\- Focus on business insights instead of raw data.



\---



\# Overall Design Approach



The dashboard follows a clean business reporting style inspired by modern executive dashboards. The design prioritizes usability, consistency, and analytical storytelling while keeping visuals simple and easy to interpret.
