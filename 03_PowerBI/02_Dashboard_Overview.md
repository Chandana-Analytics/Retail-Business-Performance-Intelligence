\# Dashboard Overview



\## Project Objective



The objective of this dashboard is to transform retail transaction data into meaningful business insights that help decision-makers understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and revenue trends.



The dashboard was developed using Snowflake SQL for data preparation and Power BI for interactive reporting.



\---



\# Dashboard Pages



\## 1. Executive Overview



Provides a high-level view of overall business performance using key business KPIs and sales trends.



Highlights:



\- Total Sales

\- Total Orders

\- Total Customers

\- Average Order Value

\- Total Quantity Sold

\- Monthly Sales Trend

\- Top Products

\- Top Countries



\---



\## 2. Customer Intelligence



Analyzes customer purchasing behavior and identifies valuable customer groups.



Highlights:



\- Repeat Customers

\- One-Time Customers

\- Repeat Customer %

\- Average Orders per Customer

\- Customer Loyalty Distribution

\- Top Customers by Revenue



\---



\## 3. Customer Segmentation Analysis



Segments customers using RFM Analysis to identify different customer groups based on purchasing behavior.



Highlights:



\- Champions

\- Loyal Customers

\- Potential Loyalists

\- At Risk Customers

\- Lost Customers

\- Big Spenders

\- Revenue Contribution by Segment



\---



\## 4. Product Intelligence



Evaluates product-level performance to identify high-performing and frequently purchased products.



Highlights:



\- Product Revenue

\- Units Sold

\- Order Volume

\- Product Search

\- Top 20 Products

\- Best Selling Products

\- Most Frequently Ordered Products



\---



\## 5. Time Intelligence



Analyzes revenue trends over time to identify seasonality and growth patterns.



Highlights:



\- Monthly Revenue

\- Month-over-Month Growth

\- Quarterly Revenue Contribution

\- Year-wise Comparison



\---



\## 6. Business Insights \& Recommendations



Summarizes the overall findings from the analysis and provides business recommendations based on the dashboard results.



Highlights:



\- Executive Summary

\- Key Business Insights

\- Business Recommendations



\---



\# Business Value



The dashboard enables business users to:



\- Monitor overall business performance.

\- Identify high-value customers.

\- Understand customer purchasing behavior.

\- Evaluate product performance.

\- Analyze revenue trends and seasonality.

\- Support data-driven business decisions.
