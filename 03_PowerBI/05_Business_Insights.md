\# Business Insights \& Recommendations



\## Executive Summary



This dashboard extends the Retail Customer Behavior Analysis SQL project by transforming the analytical findings into an interactive Power BI solution. The analysis highlights business performance across sales, customers, products, and time while enabling users to explore trends through interactive filtering and visualization.



\---



\# Key Business Insights



\## 1. Revenue is highly seasonal.



Revenue peaks consistently during Quarter 4, with October and November generating the strongest sales before a noticeable decline in December. This indicates a clear seasonal buying pattern.



\---



\## 2. Customer loyalty is a major contributor to revenue.



Nearly 72% of customers are repeat buyers, and RFM analysis shows that Champions and Loyal Customers contribute the largest share of revenue, making customer retention more valuable than constant customer acquisition.



\---



\## 3. Revenue is concentrated among a small number of products.



Only a small group of products consistently generates the highest revenue, units sold, and order volume, while the majority of products contribute relatively little to overall sales.



\---



\## 4. Revenue is geographically concentrated.



The United Kingdom contributes the overwhelming majority of sales, while revenue from other countries remains comparatively small, indicating significant dependence on one market.

\---



\# Business Recommendations



\## 1.Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns before Quarter 4 to maximize revenue during the strongest seasonal demand period.



2.Increase investment in loyalty and retention programs to strengthen relationships with Champions and Loyal Customers, who generate most of the business revenue.



3.Promote mid-performing products through targeted marketing and cross-selling to reduce dependence on a small number of high-performing products.



4.Expand successful sales strategies from the UK into other countries to diversify revenue sources and reduce geographic business risk.





\# Overall Business Value



The dashboard transforms retail transaction data into actionable business insights by combining Snowflake SQL and Power BI. It enables stakeholders to monitor business performance, understand customer behavior, evaluate product performance, and make informed decisions supported by interactive reporting.
