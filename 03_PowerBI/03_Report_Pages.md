\# 03\_Report\_Pages.md



\# Report Pages



This report contains five analytical pages, each designed to answer a different business question.



\---



\# Page 1 — Retail Sales Executive Overview



\### Objective

Provide an executive-level summary of overall sales performance.



\### KPIs

\- Total Sales

\- Total Orders

\- Total Customers

\- Average Order Value

\- Total Quantity Sold



\### Visuals

\- Monthly Sales Trend

\- Top Products by Sales

\- Top Countries by Sales



\### Business Value

Allows management to monitor overall business performance, identify revenue trends, and understand geographical contribution.



\---



\# Page 2 — Retail Sales Customer Intelligence



\### Objective

Analyze customer purchasing behaviour and identify high-value customers.



\### KPIs

\- Total Customers

\- Repeat Customers

\- Repeat Customer %

\- One-Time Customers

\- Average Orders per Customer

\- Average Quantity per Customer



\### Visuals

\- Top Customers by Revenue

\- Customer Loyalty Distribution

\- Customer Performance Summary



\### Business Value

Helps understand customer loyalty, repeat purchase behaviour, and identify valuable customers.



\---



\# Page 3 — Retail Sales Customer Segmentation Analysis



\### Objective

Analyze customer segments created using RFM Analysis performed in Snowflake SQL.



\### KPIs

\- Champions

\- Loyal Customers

\- Potential Loyalists

\- At Risk Customers

\- Lost Customers

\- Big Spenders



\### Visuals

\- Customer Segment Distribution

\- Revenue by Customer Segment

\- Top 20 Customers by Lifetime Revenue



\### Business Value

Supports customer retention strategies by identifying high-value customers and customers requiring attention.



\---



\# Page 4 — Retail Sales Product Intelligence



\### Objective

Evaluate product performance based on revenue, units sold, and order volume.



\### KPIs

\- Total Products

\- Revenue

\- Units Sold

\- Orders



\### Visuals

\- Top 20 Products by Revenue

\- Best Selling Products (Units Sold)

\- Most Frequently Ordered Products



\### Business Value

Identifies products driving revenue and highlights opportunities for product portfolio optimization.



\---



\# Page 5 — Retail Sales Time Intelligence



\### Objective

Analyze sales trends over time and identify seasonal revenue patterns.



\### KPIs

\- Selected Period Revenue



\### Visuals

\- Monthly Revenue Comparison

\- Month-over-Month Revenue Growth (%)

\- Quarterly Revenue Contribution



\### Business Value

Supports forecasting, inventory planning, and seasonal business strategy.



\---



\# Page 6 — Business Insights \& Recommendations



\### Objective

Summarize the key findings derived from the complete analysis and provide business recommendations.



\### Sections

\- Executive Summary

\- Key Insights

\- Business Recommendations



\### Business Value

Converts analytical findings into practical actions that support business decision-making.
