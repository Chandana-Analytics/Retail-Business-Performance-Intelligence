\# Business Questions Answered



The Retail Business Performance Intelligence dashboard was designed to answer key business questions that help management monitor performance, understand customer behavior, evaluate product performance, and support strategic decision-making.



\---



\# Executive Overview



\- What is the overall business revenue?

\- How many orders have been placed?

\- How many customers have made purchases?

\- What is the average order value?

\- How many products have been sold?

\- How has revenue changed over time?

\- Which countries contribute the highest revenue?



\---



\# Customer Intelligence



\- How many customers are repeat customers?

\- How many customers purchased only once?

\- What percentage of customers return to make additional purchases?

\- Which customers generate the highest revenue?

\- How many orders does an average customer place?

\- What is the average quantity purchased per customer?



\---



\# Customer Segmentation



\- Which customer segment contributes the highest business value?

\- How many customers belong to each RFM segment?

\- Which customer segments require retention strategies?

\- Which customer groups present the greatest revenue opportunity?



\---



\# Product Intelligence



\- Which products generate the highest revenue?

\- Which products are purchased most frequently?

\- Which products have the highest sales volume?

\- Which product categories contribute most to overall business performance?



\---



\# Time Intelligence



\- How does revenue change from month to month?

\- What is the month-over-month revenue growth?

\- Which quarter generates the highest revenue?

\- Are there seasonal trends in business performance?



\---



\# Business Insights \& Recommendations



\- What are the key findings from the overall analysis?

\- Which business areas require immediate attention?

\- What actions can improve customer retention?

\- Which opportunities can help increase future business performance?
