\# Business Problem



Retail businesses generate a large number of transactions every day across different customers, products, and countries. Although this information is available, it is difficult to understand overall business performance by looking at raw transaction data alone.



Business users often need answers to questions such as:



\- How is the business performing overall?

\- Which customers contribute the most revenue?

\- How many customers are repeat buyers?

\- Which products generate the highest sales?

\- How do sales change over time?

\- Which customer segments should be targeted for retention?



Answering these questions directly from transactional data requires significant manual effort and makes decision-making slow.



The objective of this project was to convert raw retail transaction data into an interactive business intelligence solution that provides clear visibility into sales performance, customer behaviour, product performance, and business trends.



To achieve this:



\- Retail transaction data was prepared and analyzed using Snowflake SQL.

\- Customer purchasing behaviour was analyzed through frequency, spending, recency, and RFM segmentation.

\- A reporting layer was created in Snowflake using fact and dimension views.

\- The reporting views were connected to Power BI, while the SQL-generated RFM segmentation was imported as a CSV file.

\- Interactive dashboards were developed to support business reporting and decision-making.



The final solution enables business users to monitor performance, identify customer trends, evaluate product performance, and make data-driven decisions through a single reporting dashboard.
