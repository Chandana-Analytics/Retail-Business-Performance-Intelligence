\# Project Outcome



The \*\*Retail Business Performance Intelligence\*\* project successfully converted SQL-based analysis into an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides a clear view of retail business performance.



The project builds on the reporting layer created in Snowflake and the RFM customer segmentation developed during the SQL phase. These outputs were integrated into Power BI to create a six-page dashboard covering Executive Overview, Customer Intelligence, Customer Segmentation, Product Intelligence, Time Intelligence, and Business Insights.



The completed dashboard allows business users to:



\- Monitor overall sales performance using executive KPIs.

\- Understand customer purchasing behaviour and customer loyalty.

\- Identify high-value, repeat, one-time, and at-risk customer segments using RFM analysis.

\- Evaluate product performance based on revenue, orders, and quantity sold.

\- Analyze sales trends across different time periods.

\- Support business decisions using interactive reports, business insights, and recommendations.



\---



\# Business Value



The dashboard transforms retail transaction data into meaningful business information that is easy to explore and understand.



Instead of manually analyzing thousands of transaction records, business users can quickly monitor sales performance, identify customer trends, evaluate product performance, and track business growth through interactive reports.



By combining \*\*Snowflake SQL\*\* for data preparation and \*\*Power BI\*\* for visualization, the project delivers a structured reporting solution that can be extended to support future analytical and business reporting requirements.
